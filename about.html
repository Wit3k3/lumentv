About LumenTV

LumenTV is a media player for Android TV, Google TV, Fire TV, Android phones and tablets. It plays the user's own sources: IPTV subscriptions the user already has (M3U and Xtream Codes playlists) and home media servers (Jellyfin, Plex, SMB, DLNA). The app provides no content of its own.

Services LumenTV works with

When the user connects an account (Settings → External accounts), LumenTV tells Trakt.tv or Simkl what is watched and reads the user's history and watchlist. Titles are matched with The Movie Database (TMDB).

LumenTV uses Trakt but is not endorsed or certified by Trakt.

LumenTV uses Simkl but is not endorsed or certified by Simkl.

This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

The same notices are shown in the app, on the Trakt.tv, Simkl and TMDB pages of Settings → External accounts.
